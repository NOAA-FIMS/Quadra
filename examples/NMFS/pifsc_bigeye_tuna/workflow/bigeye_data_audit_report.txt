and whether purse-seine age compositions look generated by a population
process or by hard-coded smooth template values.

Interpretation
--------------
If no generator source is found and the CSV age-composition values are
smooth templates, then the model is not being fit to process-generated
synthetic data. In that case persistent age-1 residuals should be treated
as a data/template mismatch until a generator is recovered or rebuilt.
