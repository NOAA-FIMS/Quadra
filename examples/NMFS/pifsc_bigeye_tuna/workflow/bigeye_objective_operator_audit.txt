High-risk items to inspect manually
-----------------------------------
A. Age composition is currently predicted from selected numbers at age.
   Verify whether the synthetic observation CSV represents selected survey/fishery
   age composition or catch-at-age composition. If it represents catch-at-age,
   the objective should use N_a * F_a/Z_a * (1-exp(-Z_a)) instead.

B. Observation timing appears to be before the annual survival/update step.
   Verify whether catches, indices, and age comps are intended to observe beginning-
   of-year population, mid-year population, or catch over the year.

C. Plus-group initialization and annual update should be checked with M and F.
   Equilibrium plus-group often uses survival into plus group divided by
   1 - survival in plus group. If annual F contributes to survival, make sure
   this is intentional and consistent.

D. Level 21 age-based M must be consistently applied in equilibrium initialization,
   catch Z, annual survival, plus group, residual diagnostics, and report writers.
